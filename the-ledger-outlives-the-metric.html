Theorem 16 · an analytic wall without an exceptional point

Cross the wall. Mix the eyes.

Proposition 4 · Theorem 10 · Corollary 11 · GEOWALL-RECIP-01 · GEOWALL-ROT-01

Only the round trip can carry a sign

A one-way transition strength is a square and has no sign. The sign lives in the round trip Q = M₊₋ M₋₊ / Δ². At a Hermitian point the return leg is the mirror of the outgoing leg and the gap is real, so Q = |M|²/Δ² ≥ 0. Open the system and the phases stop cancelling.

Theorem 6 · Corollary 7 · INERTIA-05

The full tangent space is split exactly in half

Put an idempotent in the form diag(I_k, 0). Every tangent is off-diagonal, D = [[0, X], [Y, 0]], and the trace form is q = Re Tr(XY). With U = (X + Y†)/2 and V = (X − Y†)/2 it becomes q = ‖U‖² − ‖V‖²: as many positive directions as negative, 2k(n − k) each. The self-adjoint tangents are the slice V = 0, which is why a Hermitian family never sees a negative direction.

INERTIA-05 · what a chosen direction space reports

Recorded, not recomputed: the self-adjoint control reads (48, 0, 49) on its 97 directions; the open horizon generator reads (96, 98, 0) on its 194 potential-plus-damping directions, and (86, 11, 0) on potentials alone. Among random planes of that 194-direction form, 24.0% of 2-planes and 3.2% of 3-planes read as definite, and none from dimension 5. The live census above draws planes from a toy neutral form so you can watch the same effect happen; its percentages belong to the toy.

Corollary 7: a measured form is the pullback of q to a chosen perturbation space, so its inertia belongs to that choice. The 194 directions are a specified ansatz, not the full tangent space of a 194 × 194 generator, whose real dimension is 75,272. The exact half-split is the theorem about the full space.
Theorem 1 · Theorem 8 · Corollary 9 · Corollary 16A

A negative direction pushes the overlap above the cap

For orthogonal projectors the overlap Tr(PR) is a sum of squared cosines and cannot exceed the rank. An oblique projector projects along a slanted kernel, and its overlap can. Theorem 8 turns this into a local law: Re Tr(P(0)P(t)) = k − ½ Re Tr(D²) t² + o(t²), so a direction with g(v, v) < 0 climbs above k for every small step.

Proposition 14 · Proposition 20 · Proposition 17 · CLOCK-EXPONENT-01

What the record keeps, what it forgets

Three short stories about the scalar record. A Jordan block breaks the eigenvectors but not the trace. A determinant forgets whole turns of 2πi that the trace-log keeps. And a vanishing order sets the power law you would read off as a clock.

At t = 0 the two eigenvalues meet and only one eigenvector is left. The contour around both still counts 2 and the trace-log still reads log 4. What the trace loses is the nilpotent part, which the full matrix f(J) keeps.

The wrap (Prop. 20)

With UV = e^(2πi/d) VU, the relator R = UVU†V† is e^(2πi/d) times the identity. Its determinant is exactly 1 at every d, while the sum of its eigenvalue angles is one full turn. As d grows the defect collapses; the integer stays. At d = 2 the eigenvalue sits on the branch cut and the wrap is undefined.

√|λ| grows as |t|^(m/2). A transverse crossing (m = 1) gives 1/2; a touching zero (m = 2) gives 1. The gates measured 0.5154 on the crossing case and 0.9934 on the touching case. The paper keeps these as exponents only: matching a power to a horizon lapse needs a physically fixed parameter first.

§14 · Proposition 24 · Proposition 25 · Figure 8

A screen that reads Newton up close and MOND far out

§13 · Proposition 23 · the imported form-space action

Eleven slots and one Einstein coefficient

Take the form space of a four-dimensional spacetime: scalars, one-forms and two-forms, 1 + 4 + 6 = 11 slots. Put the curvature on it and expand the trace-log: −Tr₁₁ log(I − βℝ) = 3βR + (β²/2)(R² + R_μνR^μν + R_μνρσR^μνρσ) + …. Each sector's trace is R, so the leading term is 3βR, and β = ℓ_P²/(48π) gives the Einstein–Hilbert normalization 1/(16πG).

Proposition 21 · Proposition 22

The record is not a relative entropy, and it is not the geometry

Two limits on what the scalar record can be. Proposition 21: with H = λP + μ(I − P) the projector and the selected eigenvalue can be varied separately, so the trace-log and the metric do not determine each other. Proposition 22: a same-reference relative entropy is at a minimum when the states coincide, so its first derivative is zero; the moving wall's record increment starts with slope 1/20. No constant multiple can match them.

      What this mixer is allowed to say. The finite theorems are proved within their hypotheses. The gravity bridges are conditional: the Einstein coefficient comes from an added four-dimensional action, and the Newton and MOND limits come from a separately assumed screen and mode density.
